Frequently asked questions

Why are my readings different from a sound level meter?

SecondEar shows the microphone signal level in dBFS. Every phone and tablet microphone is different, so use readings to compare places and devices. Smart owners can calibrate against a reference meter in Settings for approximate dB SPL.

Why does the Bass level look low on my speaker?

Built-in microphones are less sensitive to very deep bass (below about 50 Hz), so bass can read lower than you hear it.

Why doesn't my iPad vibrate for noise alerts?

iPads have no vibration motor. On iPad the alert is shown on screen instead.

Does SecondEar record or upload my sound?

No. Sound is processed on your device only while you measure. See the Privacy Policy.

Measuring stopped when I left the app

By design, SecondEar stops measuring when it goes to the background, so the microphone is never used without you seeing it.
