SecondEar Privacy Policy

Effective date: 19 November 2026 · App: SecondEar (iOS and Android) · Developer: Pollawat Pongkuea (THE199X)

SecondEar is a sound level meter. It is built so that your sound and your measurements stay on your device. This policy explains what the app uses and why.

Microphone

SecondEar asks for microphone access only to measure the signal level and, in Pro, its frequency content. The audio is processed in memory on your device while you measure. SecondEar does not record audio, does not save audio files and does not send audio anywhere. The microphone is used only while the app is open and measuring; it stops when you tap stop or leave the app.

Data stored on your device

The app saves the following on your device only, so it is there the next time you open the app:

  • Measurement summaries: date, duration, minimum, average and maximum level, and a small level graph (no audio).
  • Settings: theme, noise alert threshold and calibration offset.
  • Which plan you own, so the app opens unlocked without a connection.

This data is not uploaded to us or to anyone else. You can delete your history at any time with Clear history in the app, and deleting the app removes all of its data.

Purchases

Smart and Pro are one-time purchases handled entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see your name, payment card or account details. The app only receives confirmation from the store of which products your account owns, so it can unlock them and restore them.

Exporting your data

If you choose Export CSV, the app creates a file of your measurement summaries and opens your device's share sheet. Where that file goes is your choice; SecondEar does not send it anywhere by itself.

What we do not do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No advertising and no ad tracking.
  • No analytics, crash reporting or tracking SDKs.
  • No collection of contacts, location, photos or other personal data.
  • No selling or sharing of data, because we do not collect any.

Children

SecondEar is a general audience utility and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
